Helpful information

Customs Clearance Questions

Common questions before starting an import, export, or transit request.

How early should I share my documents?

Share them as early as possible so missing or inconsistent information can be identified before the cargo reaches the clearance stage.

Can you estimate my customs duties?

An estimate may be prepared using the available product, value, origin, classification, and shipment information. Final charges are subject to the applicable assessment and official requirements.

What happens if a document is missing?

The team will advise which document or clarification is needed. Missing documentation may affect processing and cargo release.

Do you handle transit cargo?

Transit arrangements can be assessed according to the route, cargo, documentation, bonds, and applicable customs procedures.
