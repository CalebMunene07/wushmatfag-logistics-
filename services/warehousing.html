How it works

From Receiving to Dispatch

Our workflow helps keep goods organized and ready for the next stage of their journey.

01

Receive the Goods

Cargo is received according to the agreed delivery instructions and checked against the available shipment information.

02

Record and Store

Goods are recorded and placed in an appropriate storage area based on their type, handling needs, and available capacity.

03

Prepare Orders

Items are selected, packed, consolidated, or prepared for transfer according to the customer's instructions.

04

Dispatch and Deliver

Outgoing goods are released for collection, onward transport, or coordinated last-mile delivery.

Helpful information

Warehouse Questions

Quick answers before requesting a storage or distribution quotation.

What information is needed for a storage quote?

Share the cargo type, number of pallets or cartons, estimated weight and dimensions, storage duration, handling requirements, and delivery frequency.

Can I store goods for a short period?

Short-term storage may be available depending on the cargo, space, handling requirements, and agreed booking arrangement.

Do you provide pick and pack services?

Pick, pack, consolidation, and dispatch support can be arranged according to the type of goods and the agreed service scope.

Can you arrange delivery after storage?

Yes. Delivery can be coordinated through suitable road transport arrangements for the selected destination.
