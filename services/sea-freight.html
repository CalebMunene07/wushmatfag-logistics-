Choose the right option

FCL or LCL?

The best option depends on your cargo volume, handling requirements, delivery timeline, and budget.

For larger shipments

FCL Shipping

Your cargo uses a container exclusively. This can provide more control over loading, sealing, and delivery planning.

  • Exclusive container use
  • Suitable for larger volumes
  • Fewer shared-cargo handling points
  • Available in 20ft, 40ft, and 40ft HC options
Request an FCL quote →
For smaller shipments

LCL Shipping

Your cargo shares container space with other shipments, making it practical when you do not need an entire container.

  • Pay for the space you use
  • Suitable for smaller cargo volumes
  • Professional consolidation support
  • Useful for flexible shipping requirements

Need to know

Sea Freight Questions

Get quick answers before requesting your shipping quotation.

What is the difference between FCL and LCL?

FCL uses a container exclusively for one shipment. LCL combines shipments from multiple customers in the same container.

Which container sizes are available?

Common options include 20ft, 40ft, and 40ft high-cube containers, subject to cargo requirements and carrier availability.

Can you arrange door-to-door delivery?

Yes. Door-to-door arrangements can be planned according to the shipment origin, destination, cargo type, and clearance requirements.

How can I track my shipment?

Use the Track & Trace page or contact the logistics team for shipment status updates.
