FTL or LTL Transport?

The right option depends on cargo volume, delivery requirements, budget, and how quickly the shipment needs to move.

For larger shipments

Full Truckload

FTL gives your shipment dedicated truck capacity for a planned movement between the pickup and delivery locations.

  • Dedicated truck space
  • Suitable for larger cargo volumes
  • Fewer handling points
  • Useful for scheduled direct delivery
Request an FTL quote →
For smaller shipments

Less-than-Truckload

LTL allows your shipment to share available truck space with other compatible cargo.

  • Shared truck capacity
  • Suitable for smaller loads
  • Flexible shipment sizes
  • Consolidation support available
Request an LTL quote →

Helpful information

Road Transport Questions

A few answers to help you prepare your shipment request.

What details are needed for a road freight quote?

We usually need the pickup location, destination, cargo description, weight, dimensions, packaging, preferred date, and any special handling requirements.

Do you support cross-border transport?

Cross-border arrangements can be planned according to the route, cargo, documentation, permits, and destination requirements.

Can I track my road shipment?

GPS tracking and shipment updates depend on the vehicle and service arrangement. Contact the team to confirm tracking availability.

Do you provide door-to-door delivery?

Door-to-door delivery can be arranged for suitable routes and cargo types, subject to the agreed quotation and operating conditions.
